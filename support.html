Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create my first invoice?
After installing the app, click on "New Invoice" and fill in the required details. Invoize will automatically generate a professional invoice that you can save as PDF or send directly via email.
Can I customize the invoice design?
Yes! Invoize offers various templates and customization options. You can add your logo, change colors and fonts, and arrange elements according to your preferences.
How does iCloud sync work?
Invoize automatically saves all your data to iCloud. Your invoices, clients, and settings will be available on all your Apple devices signed in with the same Apple ID.
Does the app support multiple currencies?
Yes, Invoize supports all major world currencies. You can set a default currency in settings or change it for each invoice individually.
How can I export my data?
You can export data in various formats including PDF, CSV, and Excel. Go to File > Export and select the desired format and date range.
